Who We Are

Where Children Discover
Their Kingdom Identity

The Chosen Generation Bible School is a children's ministry dedicated to grounding young hearts in the Word of God. Through Scripture, worship, prayer, and community, we raise children who know who they are in Christ — bold, rooted, and ready for purpose.

Scripture First

Children learn to love the Bible — through memorisation, storytelling, and age-appropriate teaching that makes the Word come alive.

Faith & Character

We build more than Bible knowledge — we nurture godly character, integrity, confidence, and a strong sense of identity in Christ.

Progressive Learning

Three structured Foundation Schools build on each other. Children grow in knowledge and depth — earning their Certification upon completion.

Safe Community

A warm, nurturing environment where children grow together in faith, friendship, and a shared love for God.

What We Offer

Our Programmes

Two flexible programme tracks designed to meet children where they are — with a path of growth that leads to full Certification.

Core Programme
Foundation School

Foundation School I, II & III

Our structured three-level programme builds a strong biblical foundation in every child. Each level deepens their understanding and walk with God.

  • Foundation School I — Knowing God
  • Foundation School II — Growing in Faith
  • Foundation School III — Living the Word
Certification awarded upon completing all three levels
Weekend Bible School

Weekend Bible School

Fun, engaging Bible sessions held every weekend. Perfect for children who want to grow in faith alongside their weekly schooling — no pressure, full of life!

  • Saturday & Sunday Sessions
  • Interactive Bible Games & Storytelling
  • Worship, Prayer & Creative Arts
Attendance certificate awarded each term

Certification of Biblical Foundation

Students who complete Foundation School I, Foundation School II, and Foundation School III receive a formal Certification of Biblical Foundation — a milestone that honours their commitment to growing in God's Word.
